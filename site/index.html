How it works

The task

Each model, running as an agent, reads a blog post and builds one 1200 by 1200 graphic in HTML. It browses the publisher's site for fonts, colours and logo, and may not look at the post's own figures.

Two prompts

Raw says only "make a tasteful graphic to go with the post". Simple adds plain instructions to show one idea, once, with nothing extra. The gap between them is how much a model needs to be told.

The grader

Claude Haiku 5.5 labels every element twice. Where the two passes differ, Claude Sonnet 5.5 breaks the tie. The rules were written from one person's hand labels on 24 graphics.
